### Needs Improvement

Although I made progress during the internship, I recognize several areas that require further improvement:

- **Discipline and time management:** I need to manage my working time more effectively and maintain greater consistency when following schedules and completing tasks.
- **Problem-solving skills:** I should develop a more systematic approach to analyzing technical problems, identifying their root causes, and evaluating possible solutions.
- **Communication skills:** I need to become more confident when presenting technical ideas, discussing issues, and reporting my progress in a professional environment.
- **Technical knowledge:** I should continue studying AWS services, CI/CD practices, and deployment automation to improve my ability to design and implement cloud-based solutions independently.

### Overall Self-Assessment

Overall, the internship was a valuable opportunity to connect the theoretical knowledge gained at university with practical cloud computing concepts and tools. It helped me better understand the CI/CD workflow and the role of AWS services in automating web application deployment.

I believe that I have achieved important learning objectives during the internship. At the same time, I understand that I still need to improve my technical expertise, problem-solving ability, communication, and time management. I will continue developing these skills to prepare myself for future work in the IT industry.
