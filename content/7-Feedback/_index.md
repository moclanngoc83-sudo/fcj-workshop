During my 10-week internship at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**, I had the opportunity to explore cloud computing, learn about AWS services, and work toward developing a practical CI/CD project. This experience helped me gain technical knowledge, improve my learning skills, and better understand the working environment in the IT industry.

The following are my personal evaluations and suggestions based on my internship experience.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The internship environment provided opportunities to learn about cloud technologies and develop practical skills. The learning activities and workshop materials helped me become more familiar with AWS services and the CI/CD process. I also had the opportunity to learn independently and explore technical topics at my own pace.

I believe that more group discussions and practical activities would make the learning environment even more engaging and help interns exchange knowledge more effectively.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**

The guidance and learning resources provided during the program helped me understand unfamiliar technologies and make progress with my internship project. The opportunity to study technical materials and receive feedback was valuable, especially when learning about AWS services and deployment workflows.

I would appreciate having more regular feedback sessions so that interns can identify their weaknesses and improve their work throughout the program.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**

The internship was relevant to my major in Information Technology, particularly the areas of computer networks, cloud computing, and software deployment.

Through the project **"Design and Implementation of an Automated CI/CD Pipeline for Web Application Deployment on AWS,"** I had the opportunity to connect my academic knowledge with practical technologies such as Git, GitHub, Spring Boot, Amazon EC2, AWS CodeBuild, AWS CodeDeploy, and AWS CodePipeline.

This experience also helped me explore cloud computing, which is an important area of modern IT infrastructure.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

During the internship, I gained a better understanding of cloud computing concepts and the CI/CD workflow. I also learned how to prepare a web application, manage source code, write unit tests, and study automated build and deployment processes.

Besides technical knowledge, I improved my self-learning, research, documentation, and problem-solving skills. These experiences helped me become more confident when approaching unfamiliar technologies.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**

The FCAJ program provided a learning environment where I could explore new technologies and develop my professional knowledge. Participating in community learning activities also gave me opportunities to listen to different perspectives and learn from other participants.

I believe that more collaborative activities, such as group projects, technical discussions, and knowledge-sharing sessions, would further strengthen connections among interns and program members.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**

The internship program provided learning opportunities and access to technical resources that supported my development. The workshops and project-oriented learning activities were particularly useful because they helped me connect theoretical concepts with practical applications.

Providing a clearer internship roadmap, regular progress reviews, and more detailed guidance on project requirements would make the experience even more effective for future interns.

---

### Additional Questions

**1. What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

The most satisfying part of my internship was having the opportunity to learn about AWS and apply my knowledge to a practical CI/CD project. Understanding how different AWS services work together to automate the deployment of a web application helped me gain a clearer picture of real-world cloud development and operations.

I also appreciated the opportunity to learn independently, explore new technologies, and gradually improve my technical skills.

**2. What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**

I think the program could be improved by providing a more detailed learning roadmap, clearer weekly objectives, and more practical exercises for each technical topic.

Regular progress reviews and feedback sessions would also help interns understand whether they are following the right direction and what they need to improve.

**3. If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they join this program? Why or why not?**

Yes, I would recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to students who are interested in cloud computing and AWS technologies.

The program offers an opportunity to explore cloud-related knowledge, work on practical learning tasks, and become familiar with technologies that are useful in the IT industry. It is especially suitable for students who are willing to learn independently and actively improve their technical skills.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

**1. Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**

I would suggest providing more hands-on exercises, practical project examples, and detailed technical documentation. It would also be helpful to organize regular feedback sessions and group discussions so that interns can share their difficulties and learn from one another.

**2. Would you like to continue this program in the future?**

Yes. I would be interested in continuing to participate in learning activities related to AWS, cloud computing, DevOps, and CI/CD. These areas are valuable for my professional development, and I would like to deepen my knowledge through more advanced projects and practical experience.

**3. Any other comments (free sharing):**

I would like to express my appreciation to the First Cloud AI Journey program for providing me with the opportunity to learn and develop my technical skills.

The internship helped me understand the importance of continuous learning, practical experience, and self-improvement in the IT industry. Although there are still many areas I need to improve, this experience has given me a clearer direction for my future learning and career development.
